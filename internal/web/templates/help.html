Help

{{.Group.GroupName}} uses assemble (opens in a new tab) to find the days everyone can get together. Each day has one session, by default {{.Group.SlotStart}}–{{.Group.SlotEnd}} ({{.Group.Timezone}}). Just tap which days you can make, and when a good match is found a manager can lock one in.

Your answers

  • {{template "status" "yes"}}YESYou can make it, and it's a preferred day for you.
  • {{template "status" "ok"}}OKYou can make it, but it's not your first choice of day.
  • {{template "status" "no"}}NOYou can't make it.
  • {{template "status" "unknown"}}?Not sure, or not answered yet. Counts as not available for locking in.

Good habits

  • Answer every day for your own schedule, not just your YES days. A day you skip shows as ?, and the group generally can't lock in a day if anyone's unsure (though a manager can override it if the group can go ahead without you).
  • Use NO sparingly. One NO rules the day out for everyone (unless a manager overrides). If you could make it at a push, say OK.
  • Add a note when there's anything to add, e.g. variations to the default time: "can't get there until 8". The whole group sees your notes.
  • You can always change your answer and note, right up until the day is over.

Locked-in days

A padlock means a manager has locked the day in. Congrats, you're all set! Each session has a message that a manager can set for timing variations, location changes etc.

Changing your availability on a locked-in day from YES or OK to NO or ? cancels it for everyone. You'll be asked first, and the whole group is notified.

Notifications

Lock-ins, cancellations and messages from the admin show at the top of your pages until you dismiss them with the cross.

Getting around

Home is the next two months at a glance. The bar shows everyone else's answers, and the icon beside it is yours. Tap a day to answer it, then swipe left or right (or use the arrows) to move between days.
