Help

{{.Group.GroupName}} uses assemble to find the days everyone can make. Each day has one session, {{.Group.SlotStart}}–{{.Group.SlotEnd}} ({{.Group.Timezone}}). Say which days you can make, and when everyone can, a manager locks one in.

Your answers

  • {{template "status" "yes"}}YESYou can make it, and you'd like to.
  • {{template "status" "ok"}}OKYou can make it, but it's not your first choice.
  • {{template "status" "no"}}NOYou can't make it.
  • {{template "status" "unknown"}}?Not sure, or not answered yet. Counts as not available for locking in.

Good habits

  • Answer every day, not just your YES days. A day you skip shows as ?, so nobody can tell whether you've thought about it.
  • Use NO sparingly. One NO rules the day out for everyone. If you could make it at a push, say OK.
  • Add a note when there's a catch, e.g. "can't get there until 8". The whole group sees your notes.
  • You can always change your answer, right up until the day is over.

Locked-in days

A padlock means a manager has locked the day in: the group is meeting. Its message says when, and notes any change of time or place.

Changing a locked-in day from YES or OK to NO or ? cancels it for everyone. You'll be asked first, and the whole group is notified.

Notifications

Lock-ins, cancellations and messages from the admin show at the top of your pages until you dismiss them with the cross.

Getting around

Home is the next two months at a glance. The bar shows everyone else's answers, and the icon beside it is yours. Tap a day to answer it, then swipe left or right (or use the arrows) to move between days.
